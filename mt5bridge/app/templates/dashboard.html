Zerodha (size in quantity):

{"action": "buy", "symbol": "RELIANCE", "exchange": "NSE", "quantity": 10, "product": "MIS"}

action is buy, sell or close (closes every position on the symbol). An alert with lots trades your MT5 accounts and one with quantity trades your Zerodha accounts. Add "reverse": true to close opposite positions first, "account": "name" to trade one account only, or "broker": "mt5" / "kite" to limit a close to one kind of account. On MT5, sl and tp are optional prices. On Zerodha, product is MIS, CNC or NRML, and "order_type": "LIMIT" with a price places a limit order. Anyone with this URL can trade your accounts, so keep it private.
